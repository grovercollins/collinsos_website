Security by architecture

Nothing leaves unless you turn it on.

One system holding everything is only safe if your firm controls where everything lives. To answer a useful question, most legal AI sends your client’s documents to a model running on someone else’s servers. That is the exposure. Collins moves the AI to the data instead of the data to the AI — matters, documents, billing, and inference all run on hardware your firm controls.

The one door open by default is your backup, encrypted before it leaves the premises. Every other outbound path is a switch, and your firm’s hand is on it.

The diagram shows a dedicated deployment — Collins on a box your firm controls outright. On the cloud edition, your firm still gets its own isolated database (never a shared tenant), the same switches, and a full export whenever you want it.
